# What is still outstanding for the ballistic computer and the MIRV bus

**A plan, not a record.** Drawn up on 2026-10-06 from four surveys of the docs, the config and the code, then
fact-checked, risk-reviewed and re-ranked by three reviews. Each item names where its evidence lives. Items come off
as they land, and the order is re-ranked when one does.

**A level, not a floor, and not yet a promise.** SHORT-RANGE.md's own bar is 3–6 flights before naming a level; only
`SOLVER SCALE 8` on Earth has flown long; 2,000–6,000 km has flown one night, two worlds a site (3fq); every night
ran at 32–63 fps on one machine, though KSA bounds the step at 33 ms on any machine (2.2); and **a median hides the shots that never land** -- a stack that breaks its own
airframe (1.11) is not in any row above. The default-on fixes of 10-07 to 10-10 rest on one to four flights each; 10-11's hold switches on five to seven holds
from one save to one target, pulsing on 112 paired rockets and two OneStage flights, and the coast's warp on one long
coast and one pad block. Anything that
reaches players or the changelog should say "a level" too.

**Seat 1 is the player's case.** It is the controlled craft, the only one KSA reports stage delta-v for, and the only
one a player flies, so every long-range number should report it on its own: 1.1-1.2 mm at 12,900 km on the shipped
stack wait, but 19-24 mm at Mato Grosso on both worlds (3fq), which is not chased.

## The release gate

**Met, and released as v0.9.5 on 2026-10-06**: Tier 0, the hover reported as "cutoff stalled" (1.1), and the held
throttle (1.2). `SaveRepairs` shipped in it and is removed on `dev` for the release after. The next release carries
1.2's trim jets, 1.1's push through a stalled throttle-down, the stack wait (1.8) scoped to the split so long shots
and walks are not held by it (1.10), the computer's targets and settings kept in the save the game actually loaded and wrote (1.4, verified in game),
a walk priced at what the bus spends with the split reserve on its later hops (4.4), an orbital hold that warps
and keeps its jets and its window (1.9, 1.13, 1.15), a coast that warps, and a stack whose throttle floor outruns its airframe
pulsed rather than broken (1.11). a launcher's release staged too early moved to the end
rather than losing the shot (1.5). 1.3, 1.12 and 1.14 are robustness a player can live without for one more version.
