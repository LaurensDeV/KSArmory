as they land, and the order is re-ranked when one does.

stack wait, but 19-24 mm at Mato Grosso on both worlds (3fq), which is not chased.

## The release gate

**Met twice: v0.9.5 on 2026-10-06** (Tier 0, the stalled throttle-down reported, the held throttle) **and v0.9.6 on
2026-10-13**, which carried everything Tier 1 has closed since: 1.1's push through a stalled throttle-down, 1.2's trim
jets, the stack wait scoped to the split (1.8, 1.10), the computer's targets and settings kept in the save (1.4), the
split reserve on a walk's later hops (4.4), an orbital hold that warps and keeps its jets and its window (1.9, 1.13,
1.15), a coast that warps, pulsing under the floor (1.11), a release staged too early moved to the end (1.5), walks
working again under the coast's warp (1.19), the handover guard (1.17), the turn into the airflow before an in-air
staging (1.18), the release kick added where it happens and a recent aim kept (1.14, 1.16), and a long coast freed at
its release gate (1.20).

**On `dev` since:** 1.21's fix, a hold that keeps its jets through a long warp to its window, and 1.12's drag-share gate and
miss cutoff, both off. LowTWR lands in millimetres at 200 and 700 km since KSA 5602 (1.12), and the arrival floor
lands from orbit too (1.3). A bus can still be backed into its spent stack after the split when the release is due
(1.22, no damage seen), so release notes should not claim a clean separation on every setting.
